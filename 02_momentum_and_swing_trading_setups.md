# Pradeep Bonde (Stockbee): Momentum and Swing Trading Setups

## 1. The 4% / 65-Day Breakout Framework

### Empirical Origin
Through extensive historical quantitative study of tens of thousands of stock market winners over multiple market cycles, Pradeep Bonde discovered a fundamental statistical truth:
> **Almost every major multi-week swing move begins with a range-expansion day of at least +4% on above-average volume, breaking out to a multi-month high (specifically a 65-day or 260-day high).**

A 65-day trading period represents approximately one financial quarter (3 months). When a stock crosses its 65-day high with range and volume expansion, it indicates that everyone who purchased the stock in the preceding quarter is now at a profit, completely eliminating overhead supply from that period.

### Technical Parameters of the 4% / 65-Day Setup
1. **Price Gain**: Current daily close (or intraday high) is **>= 4.0%** above prior close.
2. **Breakout Level**: Price breaks above the highest high of the preceding **65 trading days** (or 260 trading days for an annual breakout).
3. **Volume Expansion**: Volume is significantly higher than the 50-day moving average (at least **1.5x to 2x+** average daily volume).
4. **Range Expansion**: The daily trading range (High - Low) is noticeably wider than the average range of the previous 10-20 days.
5. **Liquidity Criteria (Dollar Volume)**:
   - Minimum Dollar Volume = Price × Volume >= **$2.5M to $5.0M per day**.
   - Ensures institutional participation, tight bid-ask spreads, and clean execution without slippage.

## 2. Anticipation & Consolidation Setups

While buying a 4% breakout works well in raging bull markets, Pradeep Bonde emphasizes **Anticipation Setups** to reduce risk, improve risk-to-reward ratios (often achieving 1:4 to 1:10), and avoid buying extended, vulnerable pivot points.

### Setup A: The Flat Top Breakout
The Flat Top is Stockbee's signature pattern for capturing explosive swing moves from structural consolidation.
- **Pattern Structure**:
  - A distinct horizontal resistance level ("flat top ceiling") tested 2 to 4 times over 2 to 8 weeks.
  - Successively **higher lows** leading into the resistance, forming an ascending triangle or tight shelf.
  - Volatility contracts as the stock nears the resistance level; daily trading candles become narrow (volatility contraction).
  - Volume diminishes during the consolidation, reflecting a lack of selling pressure.
- **Entry Strategy**:
  - *Anticipation Entry*: Buy within the base just before the breakout (1-2% below resistance) as volume begins to tick up.
  - *Breakout Entry*: Buy as the price breaches the flat top line by a few cents.
- **Stop-Loss**: Placed right below the most recent higher low or the flat shelf (typically only 1.5% to 3% below entry).

### Setup B: The 3 to 5 Day Pullback (Bull Flag)
After an explosive momentum impulse leg (often a 4% breakout or Episodic Pivot), the stock needs to digest gains before resuming higher.
- **Pattern Structure**:
  - **Impulse Leg**: Sharp, high-volume upward thrust of 10% to 30%+ over 2 to 5 days.
  - **Pullback Phase**: A quiet, orderly consolidation lasting exactly **3 to 5 trading sessions**.
  - **Volume Behavior**: Volume dries up dramatically on each pullback day, proving institutions are not dumping shares.
  - **Moving Average Support**: Price gently touches or tests the rising **8 EMA, 10 EMA, or 20 EMA**.
- **Entry Strategy**:
  - Buy when the stock breaks above the previous day's high or on the first green candle reversing off the 8/10/20 EMA.
- **Stop-Loss**: Placed below the low of the pullback day or just below the moving average.

### Setup C: The Low Cheat / Base Pocket Entry
- Rather than waiting for a full breakout at the high of a multi-month cup-and-handle or deep base, the "Low Cheat" looks for a tight 3 to 5-day pause inside the right side of the base near the midpoint.
- Allows entering with a very tight stop (1-2%), giving a huge cushion and massive R-multiples once the actual 65-day high is tested and cleared.

### Setup D: Narrow Range 7 (NR7) and Inside Days
- **Inside Day**: A session whose entire high-to-low range falls within the previous session's high-to-low range.
- **NR7**: A session with the narrowest range of the last 7 trading days.
- **Market Dynamics**: Extreme volatility contraction invariably precedes volatility expansion. When an NR7 or Inside Day forms directly at a key resistance or moving average support, Bonde places a stop-buy order just above the day's high and a stop-loss just below the day's low.

## 3. The Mechanics of Range Expansion and Volume Expansion
Pradeep Bonde summarizes the physics of swing trading into two interconnected variables:
1. **Range Expansion (RE)**: Confirms momentum, conviction, and directional thrust.
2. **Volume Expansion (VE)**: Confirms institutional footprint, liquidity injection, and institutional sponsorship.

Trading only setups exhibiting RE + VE guarantees alignment with the large smart-money flows driving the market.
