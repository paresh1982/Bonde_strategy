## 3. Classical Case Studies of Episodic Pivots

### Case 1: The Transformational Biotech FDA Approval
- **Setup**: Small-cap biotech stock consolidating at $4.00 for 9 months with zero volume.
- **Catalyst**: Overnight FDA approval for an orphan drug with projected peak sales of $500M/year.
- **Day 1 Action**: Gaps open +25% to $5.00 on 15x normal volume. First 5-minute ORB forms between $5.00 and $5.25.
- **Trade Execution**: Buy at $5.30 with stop at LOD ($5.00 - risk $0.30/share or 5.6%).
- **Outcome**: Stock closes at $5.80 (top 10% of range). Over the next 10 trading sessions, price expands to $11.00 (+100%+ gain). Half sold at Day 4 ($8.50, +10R gain); balance trailed on 10 EMA to $10.50.

### Case 2: The SaaS "Beat and Raise" Turnaround
- **Setup**: Mid-cap software stock in a stage-1 base around $35.00.
- **Catalyst**: Reports quarterly revenue growth of +85% YoY, inflects to net GAAP profitability, and raises full-year guidance by 40%.
- **Day 1 Action**: Gaps up 12% to $39.20. Consolidates in a flat top at $40.00 between 9:45 AM and 10:15 AM EST.
- **Trade Execution**: Buy breakout through $40.10 with stop at $39.20 (risk $0.90/share or 2.2%).
- **Outcome**: Stock trends steadily higher along the 10 EMA for 3 months, eventually reaching $72.00.

## 4. Influences and Recommended Reading
Pradeep Bonde's trading framework synthesizes the best elements of classical momentum pioneers:
1. **Nicolas Darvas** (*How I Made $2,000,000 in the Stock Market*): The foundation of box theory, momentum breakouts, and trailing stops.
2. **William J. O'Neil** (*How to Make Money in Stocks*): CAN SLIM methodology, institutional sponsorship, and base-building.
3. **Jesse Livermore** (*How to Trade in Stocks*): Pivotal points, progressive exposure, and trend following.
4. **Mark Minervini** (*Trade Like a Stock Market Wizard*): Specific Entry Point (SEPA) and Volatility Contraction Pattern (VCP).
